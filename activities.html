Activities FAQ

Paradise Bay Resort offers a variety of activities including swimming, kayaking, snorkeling, jet skiing, beach volleyball, sunset cruises, cycling and fishing.

Swimming and beach volleyball are available free of charge for resort guests. Other activities may have separate charges.

Advance booking is recommended for popular activities such as kayaking, snorkeling, jet skiing and sunset cruises.

Yes. Several activities are suitable for families and children. Our staff can help guests choose activities based on age and safety requirements.

The sunset cruise and beach walk are excellent choices for enjoying the evening and watching the beautiful sunset over the ocean.

Guests should bring comfortable swimwear, sunscreen, towels and suitable footwear. Safety equipment is provided for applicable activities.
